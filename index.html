AquaClear: Microplastic Storm Drain Filters

About AquaClear

Microplastic flow from roads are a serious issue that can cause harm to both marine enironments and human health. Our filters use a biotextile to filter out the microplastics in rainwater run off before they enter the storm drain system. Most storm drains lead directly into natural water sources so stopping the microplastics at the drains is a key way to remove microplastics from water sources
